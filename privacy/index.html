Privacy Policy

Bodytwin: Health Simulator · Effective 24 October 2026

In short
  • Your twin runs on your iPhone. Data read from Apple Health stays on the phone and is never uploaded as raw records.
  • An account is optional. With one, we store the lab reports you upload, the values read from them and your consent choices, in the EU.
  • Nothing is sent to an AI service until you switch it on, and you can switch it off at any time.
  • We never sell your data, never use it for advertising and never track you across apps.
  • Deleting your account in the app deletes your data from our servers right away.

Who we are

Bodytwin: Health Simulator (“Bodytwin”, “we”) is published by Izat Bekboev, an individual developer, who is the controller of the personal data described here. Contact: ibekboev@gmail.com.

Data that stays on your iPhone

With your permission, Bodytwin reads Apple Health data such as sleep, heart rate, heart rate variability, activity, workouts, body measurements, nutrition, sex and date of birth. The app turns these into the views and verdicts you see. These records are processed on the device and are not sent to our servers. Data from Apple Health is never used for advertising or marketing, never sold, and never stored in iCloud by us.

AI features

AI features are optional, need your explicit consent and are part of the subscription. Each has its own switch in More → Account, and turning it off stops any further sending.

  • Reading lab reports. The report you choose is sent to Google’s Gemini API to read its values.
  • Assistant and simulations. Your question or scenario, a short summary of your twin computed on the phone (for example average sleep, resting heart rate trend, verdicts per area) and your confirmed lab values are sent to Google’s Gemini API. Raw Apple Health records are not sent.
  • Research for simulations. To check a scenario against published research, the scenario text is used to run Google Search queries. Your health data is not part of those queries. Research notes found for a scenario may be reused for anyone asking the same scenario for up to 30 days; they are stored without any link to you.

We use Google’s paid Gemini API service. Under its terms Google does not use this data to train its models; it may keep it for a limited period to detect abuse. AI answers are general information, not medical advice and not a diagnosis.

Legal bases (EU and UK)

  • Health data, including lab reports and anything sent to AI features: your explicit consent (GDPR Art. 9(2)(a) and 6(1)(a)).
  • Your account and subscription: performing our contract with you (Art. 6(1)(b)).
  • Usage limits and preventing abuse: our legitimate interest in a secure, affordable service (Art. 6(1)(f)).

You can withdraw consent at any time in the app; this does not affect processing that already happened.

How long we keep data

  • Account data, lab reports and values: until you delete them or your account.
  • When you delete your account, your records and files are deleted from our database and storage immediately. Encrypted database backups are overwritten within 7 days.
  • Shared research notes: up to 30 days.
  • Your subscription history stays with Apple and RevenueCat as required for purchases and tax. Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription; cancel it in Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions.

Your rights

You can access, correct, export and delete your data, restrict or object to processing, and withdraw consent. Most of this is in the app: lab values can be corrected, a report can be deleted together with its values, AI features can be switched off, and your account can be deleted in More → Account. For anything else, write to ibekboev@gmail.com; we reply within one month. You can also complain to your data protection authority (in the UK, the ICO).

US residents: we do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising, and we treat health data as sensitive data used only to provide the features you ask for.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Every record is readable only by its owner, enforced in the database. Keys for AI services are kept on our servers, never in the app.

Children

Bodytwin is not intended for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If we change how we handle data, we will update this page and its date. If a change needs new consent — for example a new AI feature or provider — the app will ask before anything is sent.
